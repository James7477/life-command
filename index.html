📅 Calendar backup preference

Life Command stores each appointment in the encrypted vault. Calendar links or .ics files are created only when you tap Add to Calendar. You must confirm the second copy in your calendar app. Phone unlock and Life Command vault passwords are separate.

📖 Spoken answers and private history

Life Command keeps a record of the things you say or type to it and its answers while the vault is unlocked. Ask about last week or work hours; enter your private-history PIN to hear a summary. Turn on Read responses aloud above to hear it spoken.

Example: Say “Remind me to stop by the store after work.” We'll schedule it before your configured work end time. The prototype only notifies you while its browser page is open and unlocked; it doesn't track your departures or wake the phone automatically.

Optional browser speech recognition may use a cloud speech service. Do not use voice capture for confidential information on shared devices.

Privacy controls

Your vault password and private-history PIN belong to Life Command, not your phone. They can be entirely different from your phone's screen-lock PIN or biometrics. This browser test cannot unlock with Face ID or fingerprint.
